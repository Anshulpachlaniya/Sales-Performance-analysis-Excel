# Multi-Channel IT Hardware & Electronics Sales Dashboard (2026)

![Dashboard Preview](sales_dashboard_overview.png)

## Project Overview
An interactive Microsoft Excel analytics project evaluating **2,000 sales transactions ($2.45M net revenue)** across 5 regions, 4 sales channels, and 6 IT hardware/office product categories. This project transforms raw transactional order logs into an executive-ready dashboard to track KPIs, seasonal trends, top-performing SKUs, and revenue leakage caused by promotional discounting.

---

## Business Problem
Leadership needed visibility into:
1. Which product categories, individual SKUs, and sales channels generate the highest revenue versus unit volume.
2. How much top-line revenue is lost to promotional discounting across B2B and B2C channels.
3. Seasonal revenue fluctuations, regional benchmarks, and the value gap between new and returning customers.

---

## Key Metrics (2026 Full Year)
Net Revenue: $2,452,084.30
Gross Pre-Discount Sales: $2,650,950.82
Total Discount Loss: $198,866.52 (7.50% effective discount rate)
Total Orders: 2,000
Total Units Sold: 5,552
Average Order Value (AOV): $1,226.04

---

## Key Analytical Findings
Hardware Concentration: Computers drive 54.91% ($1.35M) of total revenue from just 8.68% of unit volume, while Accessories generate the highest unit volume (1,615 units) but only 2.97% ($72.90K) of revenue. The top 5 SKUs—led by Gaming Laptop ($432.58K) and Laptop Air 13 ($273.53K)—account for 57.73% of all revenue.
Channel Economics: Online leads total revenue ($791.54K, 32.28%), while Corporate Sales achieves the highest Average Order Value at $2,158.39 (more than 2.5x Marketplace at $838.93).
Discount Leakage: Computers account for $110.98K (55.81%) of all discount losses. Meanwhile, Marketplace has the steepest average discount rate (8.36%) despite delivering the lowest AOV.
Customer Retention: Returning customers generate 68.65% ($1.68M) of total revenue and spend 15.08% more per order ($1,284.00 AOV) than new customers ($1,115.76 AOV).
Seasonality & Regions: Revenue peaks in November ($274.37K) and June ($258.66K) and dips in July ($150.71K) and May ($153.64K). North ($561.75K)** and Central ($556.71K) are the top-performing regions.

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. Tighten Discount Controls on High-Ticket Hardware: Implement approval thresholds for discounts exceeding 8% on Computers and audit Marketplace discounting (8.36% avg.) to recover margin leakage.
2. Cross-Sell High-Volume Accessories: Bundle top-moving Accessories (1,615 units) with laptop and monitor orders to increase Online and Retail Store basket sizes.
3. Scale B2B Corporate Sales in West & South:** Expand corporate account outreach in West ($421.32K) and South ($444.49K) using the high-AOV ($2,158.39) Corporate Sales model.
4. Target Off-Peak Months (May & July): Run B2B hardware refresh campaigns and returning-customer promotions ahead of May and July to offset seasonal slowdowns.
